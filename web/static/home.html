    Data and method

    Where the numbers come from

    Option chains

    SPX and SPXW option quotes at the 16:15 ET options close (13:15 on half days), from 2020 on.

    Implied and actual move

    The implied move is the at-the-money straddle at the previous 16:15 close, as a share of the forward. The actual move is the change from that forward to the release-day close.

    Baselines

    An event is compared with the median implied move of ordinary days within 20 trading days either side, and with … pairs of consecutive ordinary days.

    Flagged days

    … of … daily surfaces are flagged: their fitted smiles miss by more than 0.5 vol points somewhere, or total variance falls with expiry. Results are shown with and without them. … trading days have no surface.

    Frozen v1 and live

    Everything on this page is frozen v1, data to …, as recorded in findings_v1.md. In the terminal, rows added later by the daily update are marked live, and a premium still missing its following days is marked provisional.

    Honest by design

    What we don't claim

    • Weak results stay labelled weak

      The payrolls premium was … pp in 2023–26 (n …) but only … pp in 2020–23 (n …). It did not hold out of sample, so it is shown as not holding (Finding 1.3).

    • One event is not a pattern

      A single release day proves nothing on its own. Results are medians over many events, with their n beside them, and … comparisons were tested, so one could pass by chance.

    • S&P 500 options only

      One index, one data vendor, 2020 to 2026. Nothing here says a position would have made or lost money after spreads, fees, slippage or margin.

    • Four checks

      An out-of-sample period added with the method frozen; ordinary-day controls; vol measured at a fixed strike; and an independent recheck from the raw chains with separate code.
